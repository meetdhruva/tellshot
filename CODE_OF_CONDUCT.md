# Code of conduct

Tellshot follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the work,
not the person. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@meetdhruva](https://github.com/meetdhruva), through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break this code, and may ban
contributors temporarily or permanently, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
